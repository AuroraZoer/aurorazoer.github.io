About Me

I study how the human brain processes language. My current research uses large language models to investigate how language abilities are represented and how those representations relate to language difficulties in aphasia. I work at the State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems.

Research Interests

  • Computational neuroscience and cognitive science
  • Human language processing and its neural basis
  • Neuroimaging and large language models for studying language cognition
